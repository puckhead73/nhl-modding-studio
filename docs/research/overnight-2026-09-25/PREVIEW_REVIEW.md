## Review guidance

- Raw copies exist for every trimmed copy (same basename, `__raw.wav`): the first 1152 samples of an EAL3 raw file are encoder warm-up and are expected to sound wrong; XMA raw = trimmed.
- Loop copies are stub + body + body; the loop point is at the stub length (29 samples PS3 / 24 samples X360) and at stub + body.
- 4-channel material is presented as two stereo files (`_s0`, `_s1`) because the de-interleave rule is inferred (frame order + granule-index alternation), not reference-validated.
- Nothing here says what the console mixes; the previews are decoder output.
