## 2. Unanswered questions added by this lane

- **Q-CSV-1.** Which stored row a PS4 16–21 creation screen reads (`DISPLAY_*` context, `DEFAULT_*_VALUES` context,
  `_DEFAULT_` row, `setting.csv` cell) and whether `is_enabled`/`is_visible`/`usage` gate it — card P1BS-CSV-1.
- **Q-CSV-2.** Whether the PS4 loader accepts a re-encoded chunkzip member (byte-faithful or not) and a retargeted
  (appended) row — card P1BS-CSV-1 treatment D on NHL 17.
- **Q-CSV-3.** EA's raw-vs-chunkzip storage threshold for small members (raw ≤ 519 B, compressed ≥ 2 319 B observed).
- **Q-R2-1.** BIG4 loader tolerance of append-and-retarget (non-monotonic offsets) vs ordered reflow, and whether the `.bh` sidecar is consulted — card R2-BIG4-1.
- **Q-R2-2.** Xbox 360 loader tolerance of a `chunkzip` member in place of `chunklzx` — card R2-LZX-1.
- **Q-R2-3.** Whether the PS4 loader honours `archive_size` (four shipped archives declare less than their length; rev. 2 — rev. 1 said ten).
- **Q-R2-4.** EA's RefPack match finder (Studio's streams are longer on every vector).
- **Q-R3-1.** Gen7 league → `divisionlogos`/`trophies` id mapping (computed in game code; the user's visual check of `previews/contact-nhllegacy-x360-{divisionlogos,trophies}.png` labels the ids).
- **Q-R4-1.** Which runtime input feeds the compiled `League_is_*` / `LEAGUE_nhl_or_ahl` conditions (league id, cup type, game mode) and whether non-NHL/AHL leagues fall into `generic_NOT_AHL_or_NHL` — card R4-AUD-1 (listen-only).
- **Q-R4-2.** SND10 `SBle`/`MOIR` bank index (shared with handoff 19 §2) and per-platform sample codecs.
- **Q-R5-1.** How NHL 07 labels its three European leagues at runtime (raw key / blank / logo only) — card R5-07-1.
- **Q-R6-1 (resolved at the data level by handoff 07).** PS4 on-ice/stadium assets are slug-named; the binding is in `gccollections.vlt` as typed attributes/references (arena `ID`, centre-logo `Team`, jersey `Team`/`UniformVariation`/`JerseyFit` → `ge_texture.FileRef`). Earlier text: Candidate resolver: the Attrib/VLT vault `boot.big:nhl/data/common/database/attrib/gccollections.{vlt,bin}` (`ErtS` = the `.bin` string/blob pool; 10–25 MB decoded; id-keyed `centerlogo_{artid}_texpack` / `centerline_{id}_texpack` collection names plus slug-named stadium/jersey/mascot names co-occur there) — parsed references are the bounded next item (handoff 07; rev. 2 wording).
- **Q-R6-2.** NHL 20/21 jersey thumbnail fourth component (0–19, 2–8 per triple) and the `goalsongid`/`winsongid`/`powerplaysongid` id space.
- **Q-R3-2.** Consumers of PS4 `league_icon_#`, `rostermoves/logo-#` (not in UI text; `.gfx`/executable unsearched) and the `[loc FRE_FR]` fallback when a `_fr` twin is missing.

- **Q-R7-1.** Collection-key derivation (not `lookup2` of the `DebugName`), the per-record `ExpN` key, and the numbered sibling-vault file names.
- **Q-R7-2.** The selection rule when a `(Team, UniformVariation, JerseyFit)` triple has two jersey packs (24–88 triples on 17–21), and NHL 21's `variant` 10 / `style` 2 rows without a pack.
- **Q-R7-3.** Whether the game consumes the vault for these bindings at runtime and what an edited `Team`/`ID` attribute or `RefSpec` does (no card proposed; no writer exists).
- **Q-R7-4.** The 2 747 unresolved attribute names and the enum value names; `ge_nhl_skaterhead*` (766 records) not decoded — the bespoke head binding stays open.
