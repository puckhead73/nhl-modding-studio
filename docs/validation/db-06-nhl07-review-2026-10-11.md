## Mapping and limits

NHL07 uses `StEO` (exhibitionarena) as a team-indexed selector: `RREB` chooses stock/custom and `buDa` identifies the underlying arena's `vfEq` key. Stock fields live in `cubz`, not the selector. The original source has 93 resolved selectors referring to 92 distinct stock records; the stock table contains 96 records. Inventory is reproducible with `cargo run -p studio-api --example nhl07_db06_inventory`.

Assignment labels prove which database option another venue uses, not that a particular texture, model or audio consumer honors it. Asset replacement remains AA-02/AUD-01. Ice quality, crowd type and crease behavior are unqualified and read-only. New arena creation/duplication is unavailable: the custom table and capacity metadata do not establish safe registration, required assets or game-mode support. No creation support is claimed.

Only reviewed native effects become qualified. All appearance combinations, capacity effects, crowd density, commentary playback and existing Season/Dynasty saves require their own evidence. Other games/platforms do not inherit NHL07 acceptance.
