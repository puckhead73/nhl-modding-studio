## Implemented

- Named NHL conference/division selectors and league hierarchy. League swaps exchange teams and translate every affected schedule slot by team identity. All-Star organization is protected.
- Team Schedule calendar: template and display-year selectors, home/away, opponent names and available logos, add/edit/remove, rescheduling, conflict/stale-dialog refusal, immediate feedback and rollback. Both teams read one shared fixture.
- Seven NHL07 source templates support insertion/deletion, packed-table updates, CRCs, Undo/Redo, save/reopen, developer archives and release installation/restoration.
- League CSV export/import carries template identity, month/day, stable team IDs and readable names. BOM, CRLF and quoted Unicode/multiline names work. Wrong templates, invalid dates, unresolved opponents and newly introduced double bookings are refused.
- Round-robin and pair/group generation support changed game counts. Non-participant fixtures retain dates/opponents/settings; unchanged-length generations also preserve their row positions.
- Overview groups schedules by template. **Organization and schedules are one linked restoration group: its × restores all membership and schedule changes together.** A schedule-only × restores that template. Both are undoable; other player/team edits remain.
- Mutations join the Save/Build/Close queue; native work runs off the UI thread.
