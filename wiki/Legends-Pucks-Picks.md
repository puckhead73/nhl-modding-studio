# Legends: Puck's Picks

**Legends › Puck's Picks** builds a best-ever roster for every NHL franchise from the historical database, ranking players on a **Legends rating**, the EA overall formula run over each attribute's three best seasons under the active formula profile. In the current beta it is a browser: there is no path yet from a Legends team into a game, and lines and strategies are not built.

## Before anything shows

The rankings exist only after a **Legends calculation** for the active profile. Run it from the prompt after saving a profile on [Formulas](Formulas), or from the app-wide banner ("Legends ratings haven't been calculated for <profile>…" / "…were built against a different corpus…") with **Calculate now**. The run reads every player's every season and reports "Rated N of M players in Ns." Switching or saving a profile without recalculating brings the banner back; **Not now** hides it until the next profile change.

## The list

Every NHL franchise, with abbreviation and the forward, defence and goalie counts once calculated. Two Legends-only teams appear after calculation: **European Elite** ("The best European players who missed a franchise team — no NHL games required…") and **Windsor Legends** (the North American counterpart, reaching WHA, NHA and other-league greats).

## The team page

The banner shows the club's colours, name, classification and roster count. **Season Identity** chooses which era's colours, name and crest the built team wears; "Sets the colours, name and crest only — never the roster."

Tabs:

- **Team Data**: identity, classification and colours for the chosen season identity; the crest is "Pending the logo database". Legends-only teams show their eligibility rules instead.
- **Depth Chart**: the five position blocks with each player's Legends overall, tier-coloured. If ratings are missing the page says "Roster unavailable." with the reason.
- **Edit Lines**: "Line editing isn't built yet."
- **Jersey Numbers & Captaincy**: the same panels as a historical team, with the note that Legends players carry no number or captaincy until placed onto an EA team.
- **Strategy**: "Strategy isn't built yet."

To put any of these players into a game today, send their seasons from [Historical Players](Historical-Players) to the [Roster Builder](Roster-Builder).
