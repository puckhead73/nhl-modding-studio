# Texture Lab

**Assets › Texture Lab** is a placeholder in the current beta: "Browse, preview and replace the install's textures, plus team logos, equipment and audio. Arriving in a later phase."

What it will gather already exists piecemeal elsewhere:

- single-file texture replacement in the [File Browser](File-Browser);
- team logos on the team's **Team Data** tab ([Teams](Teams)) and league logos on the league page ([Leagues](Leagues));
- kit textures on **Uniforms** ([Uniforms and Nameplates](Uniforms-and-Nameplates));
- equipment textures in the [Hockey Shop](Hockey-Shop);
- audio listing and previews in the [Commentary and Speech Catalogue](Commentary-and-Speech-Catalogue).
