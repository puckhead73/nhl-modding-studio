# NHL Modding Studio

**NHL Modding Studio** is a Windows desktop toolkit for modding EA's NHL games on Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4 and the NHL Legacy Recompilation. It is the spiritual successor to NHLViewNG: one application that edits the games' databases, builds and installs mod packages, edits save files, imports players and teams from a historical hockey database that reaches back to 1909, and works with the games' graphical and audio assets.

> **Status: beta, not released.** The Studio is in active development and shared with testers only. Every title and platform listed as supported is *implemented and covered by automated tests* against reference data; **no title is yet verified in the game itself**. Pages in this wiki say which of those two levels a feature has reached. See the [Contributing to the Wiki](Contributing-to-the-Wiki) page for the words we use.

## Two ways in

**Use the Studio.** Start with [Requirements and Installation](Requirements-and-Installation) and the [Quick Start](Quick-Start-Edit-a-Player-and-Build-a-Mod), which walks from a fresh project to an activated mod. The *Modding Studio reference*, *Mod Manager and packages* and *Save Editor* sections of the sidebar describe every screen and action; [Core Concepts](Core-Concepts) explains the ideas behind them.

**Learn how the games work.** The [Modding Knowledge Base](Knowledge-Base-Home) collects what has been established about the EA NHL series: the TDB database format, save containers, archives, textures, meshes, audio, localization and how each title differs from its neighbours. Every claim there carries an evidence label explained in [Evidence and Verification Conventions](Evidence-and-Verification-Conventions).

## Games covered

| Platform | Titles |
|---|---|
| Xbox 360 | NHL 07 through NHL 15, NHL Legacy |
| PlayStation 3 | NHL 08 through NHL 15, NHL Legacy |
| PlayStation 4 | NHL 15 through NHL 21 |
| NHL Legacy Recompiled | NHL Legacy (Xbox-derived recompilation) |

That is 17 catalogue entries and 27 title-and-platform targets. There is no Xbox One target. NHL 22, 23 and 24 on PlayStation 4 open in a separate read-only asset browser.
